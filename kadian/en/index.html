OnTime Clock · Help & Support

FAQ

Where is my data stored?
Your sales and settings stay only on your phone; we have no servers. They are deleted when you delete the app.

Does it cost anything?
Basic features are free, with up to 3 sales at a time. Membership gives you no ads and unlimited sales: Lifetime is ¥18; Quarterly is ¥8 and renews every 3 months until you cancel it in system Settings. The purchase page shows the actual price.

Will my membership move to a new phone?
Membership follows your Apple Account. Open the membership page on the new phone and tap Restore Purchases.

Why does it differ from the countdown on the sale page?
A sale page’s countdown may be calculated by your phone and can be off by about a second. OnTime Clock syncs each sale with its platform’s server time. If it can’t sync, the card says so and the countdown uses your phone’s time.

Will it buy for me?
No. OnTime Clock only helps you time it; you place the order yourself.
