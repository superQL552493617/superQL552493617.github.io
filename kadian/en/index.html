FAQ

Where is my data stored?
Everything stays on your phone; we have no servers. You can export a backup in the app.

Does it cost anything?
All features are free, with no ads.

Why does it differ from the countdown on the sale page?
The sale page’s countdown is calculated by your phone and can be off by about a second. OnCue syncs with the platform’s server, so once you pick the right platform, go by OnCue. If it can’t sync, the screen says so and shows your phone’s time.

Will it buy for me?
No. OnCue only helps you time it; you place the order yourself.
